Forgot your password?

Password resets are handled by the choir admin to keep your account secure.

1Contact the choir admin or an executive committee member.
2Give them your registered email or PF number.
3They'll reset it to the default password.
4Log in with the default password — you'll be prompted to set your own immediately.
Once your password is reset, sign in with UOEMCHOIR and choose a new one on the next screen.
